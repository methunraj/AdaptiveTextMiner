QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT
XYZ Corporation
Q3 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

XYZ Corporation continued to demonstrate strong financial performance in the third quarter of 2024, with revenue growth of 12.5% year-over-year and an EBITDA margin improvement of 1.8 percentage points. Our strategic initiatives in product innovation and market expansion have yielded positive results, positioning us well for sustained growth in the coming quarters.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

• Revenue increased to $245.8 million, up 12.5% compared to Q3 2023
• Gross profit margin expanded to 58.3%, a 1.1 percentage point improvement
• EBITDA increased 18.7% to $78.3 million
• Net income reached $42.5 million, representing a 14.2% increase
• Earnings per share (EPS) of $1.28, up from $1.12 in Q3 2023
• Free cash flow grew by 22.3% to $65.2 million
